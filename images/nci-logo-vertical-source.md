# NCI vertical logo

`nci-logo-vertical.png` is the unmodified image supplied by Lu Mao on September 25, 2026, selected at his request for the Research page's vertical agency-logo layout.

The artwork matches the English Social Media Logo (figure 7A) in NCI's February 2018 Branding and Logo Use Guidelines, printed page 8 (PDF page 10):
https://policyviz.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/NCI-Branding-and-Logo-Use-Guide_2.pdf

This is the square social-media variant, not the horizontal enterprise website wordmark. Its red panel and lettering are preserved. The original horizontal NCI SVG is retained separately in case a future layout needs it.
