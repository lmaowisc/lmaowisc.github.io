# NCI logo source

Official NIH/NCI mobile wordmark downloaded from the National Cancer Institute website on September 25, 2026:

https://www.cancer.gov/sites/www/files/ncids_header/logos/Logo_NCI_Mobile.svg

The SVG artwork, lettering, colors, and proportions are unchanged. Its viewBox was tightened from `0 0 288 38` to `0 0 210 38` to remove unused transparent space on the right for the funding-agency column.
